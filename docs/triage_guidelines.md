# Incident Triage & Escalation Guidelines
##Severity Classification
**CRITICAL:** Active Ransomeware encryption or administrative infrastructure compromise.
* **HIGH:** Individual corporate account compromise or isolated malware infection.
* **LOW:** Brute-force reconnaissance noise or standard policy alerts.

  ##Escalation Path
  1.First Responder fills out `incident_report_template.md`.
  2. Severity categorized by Core Analyst.
  3. High/Critical events escalated to senior tier stakeholders within 15 minutes.
